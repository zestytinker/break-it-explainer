Break-it explainers

Each page teaches one tech idea by letting you build a tiny version of it, then break it. No setup, nothing to install, just click around.

Every explainer follows the same six steps:
  1. Why the problem exists
  2. How the trick works, hands on
  3. Break it, and see the trade-off
  4. Who invented it and for what
  5. Where it lives today
  6. Where to read further
